How long is Final Fantasy VII?
When focusing on the main objectives, Final Fantasy VII is about 36 Hours in length. If you're a gamer that strives to see all aspects of the game, you are likely to spend around 78½ Hours to obtain 100% completion.
Final Fantasy VII will take you approximately 5 Weeks to complete if you play for 1.5 hours a day while focusing on some extra content.
